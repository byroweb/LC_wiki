Player vs player

Two players, built the same way as on the equipment page, and the damage going both ways between them — with the two things a fight between players does differently: the defender's own defence roll, and a protection prayer that cuts the hit rather than stopping it.

What the scripts say about building for player-killing

Only your best attacking style counts toward combat level. [proc,player_combat_level] adds defence, hitpoints and half of prayer, then only the largest of three offence terms: 13 × (attack + strength), 13 × 1.5 × ranged, or 13 × 1.5 × magic. A melee pure can therefore raise ranged and magic to two thirds of attack plus strength without the offence term moving off melee. It is not quite free: every point of ranged damage also gives hitpoints xp, so the hitpoints estimate on the panels rises with ranged. Magic trained by casting is mostly the spell's base xp, which gives no hitpoints. Load the tribrid pure levels and watch the combat level against the plain pure.

Magic defence is mostly magic level. [proc,player_combat_stat] builds the magic defence roll from seven tenths of magic level and three tenths of defence. A pure with 1 magic and 1 defence is the easiest target there is for spells, and its free magic levels are its cheapest protection. Rune armour carries negative magic defence bonuses, so a tank in plate leans on its own magic level even more. Magic attacks are not modelled on this page, so this is the one side of these fights the numbers above do not show.

Against 1 defence, accuracy costs almost nothing. The defender's roll is their effective defence times their defence bonus plus 64. At 1 defence it stays small whatever they wear, so an attacker's hit chance against them barely moves with attack bonus. That lets an attacker wear plate, with its ranged attack penalties, for the defence it gives against the pure's own hits.

Rapid takes a tick off every throw. [label,pvp_ranged_attack] knocks one tick off the attack delay on the rapid style. Rune knives are one-handed, so the kiteshield stays on, and on rapid they throw faster than a shortbow fires. The ranged tank puts these together: rune plate and a kiteshield for defence, black dragonhide chaps and vambraces where rune has nothing better for ranged, and knives on rapid. Compare it with each pure using the buttons at the top.

The price is combat level. Each defence level is a quarter of a combat level, against about a third for attack or strength and about a half for ranged or magic. [proc,pvp_level_check] only lets two players fight when their combat levels differ by no more than the wilderness level, so a heavier account meets low pures only deeper in. A protection prayer cuts a hit to six tenths rather than stopping it, and Protect from Magic halves how long a bind, snare or entangle holds.
