How it was built

NPC, item, floor and scenery definitions come from the .npc, .obj, .flo and .loc configs; spawns and terrain from the .jm2 map files; drop tables by interpreting the RuneScript death handlers ([ai_queue3,...]); shops from .inv configs; quest involvement from identifier references inside the quest scripts. The raw graph is in data/graph.json.
